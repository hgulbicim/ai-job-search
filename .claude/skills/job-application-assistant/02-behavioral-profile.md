## Overview
Hüseyin's working pattern is a **pragmatic builder-architect**: someone who takes technical
ownership of complex systems, prefers shipping working solutions over theoretical completeness,
and has repeatedly stepped into leadership without leaving the code behind. The career arc is
consistent: individual contributor, to team lead, to principal, with hands-on architecture
throughout. His own CV summary leads with "fast learner, practical problem solver, always open
to learning new technologies", and 13 years of stack breadth backs that up.

## Core Behavioral Drives

| Drive | Level | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Technical ownership | High | Repeatedly given architecture and technology-decision authority (Softtech architect, Hepsiburada principal). Wants to own outcomes, not just tickets. |
| Pragmatism over purism | High | Career built on legacy modernisation: C++ to .NET Core, WinForms to Angular, WCF to microservices. Comfortable in imperfect systems, works incrementally. |
| Breadth-seeking / learning drive | High | Full stack, multiple languages (C#, Go, Node.js), four front-end frameworks, extensive certification record. Boredom is a real risk in a narrow role. |
| Collaboration and mentoring | Moderate-High | Led an 11-person cross-functional team including product and QA. Coordination is a demonstrated strength, not an aspiration. |
| Formal process adherence | Moderate | Works fluently in Agile and in regulated contexts (PCI DSS, banking), but the record suggests he optimises process rather than following it ceremonially. |

## Strongest Behaviors
- **Scaling under pressure:** owned systems at 250M+ requests/day and built payment gateways
  for four major banks. High-stakes, high-traffic environments are where the strongest stories
  live.
- **Bridging technical and business:** an MBA plus principal-level engineering. Comfortable
  arguing a technical decision in business terms, which is exactly what architect and lead
  interviews probe for.
- **Modernising without rewriting:** the repeated pattern of moving legacy systems to modern
  architectures while they stay in production.
- **Cross-functional leadership:** ran backend, frontend, QA, and product in one team. Not just
  managing engineers.

## How You Work Best
- Environments with **real scale or real complexity**. Low-traffic CRUD work will not hold his
  attention.
- **Autonomy over technical decisions**, with accountability for the result.
- **English-speaking or mixed international teams.** Four years of remote work across borders
  (İstanbul team while based in Salzburg) shows this works well.
- **Hybrid or remote-heavy.** Long, fully-onsite commutes are a friction point given the
  Salzburg base.

## Mapping to Job Posting Language

**Strong behavioural fit** when a posting says:
- "architecture", "technical ownership", "system design", "technical direction"
- "high traffic", "scalability", "distributed systems", "high availability"
- "legacy modernisation", "migration", "greenfield rebuild"
- "mentoring", "cross-functional", "product collaboration"
- "English is our working language", "international team"
- "autonomy", "you decide how", "flat hierarchy"

**Potential friction** (flag, not a deal-breaker):
- "Deutsch erforderlich", "German C1", customer-facing German - this is a **Language Gate FAIL**,
  not friction
- "maintenance of existing applications" as the dominant description of the role
- heavily prescriptive process, "follow established specifications", no design input
- fully onsite, five days, outside the commute radius
- very small companies with no engineering peers to bounce architecture off

## Management Style Preferences
- **Works with:** managers who set outcomes and get out of the way; direct feedback; being
  trusted with technology decisions.
- **Does not work with:** micromanagement of implementation detail; decisions made without
  engineering input and handed down as fixed specifications.

  bank VPOS gateways, 11-person team), then connect it forward to the employer's problem. One
  example, specific numbers, no adjectives.
- **CV:** emphasise architecture and ownership over tool lists. The tools are already dense;
  what differentiates is that he decided things, not just used things.
- **Interviews:** the reliable STAR set is (1) the C++ to .NET Core migration at Softtech,
  (2) scaling the customer-service platform at Hepsiburada, (3) leading 11 people across four
  disciplines, (4) PCI DSS-compliant payment gateway delivery.
- **Don't overstate:** no CS degree, no German, no formal behavioural assessment, no
